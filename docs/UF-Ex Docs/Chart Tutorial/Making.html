Charting with UF-Ex

This tutorial will go over fundemental concepts you will utilize in creating charts with UF-Ex, we will create two charts that act as an interactive example for various concepts you will learn throughout this and future tutorials. When completed, the finished products can equip you with the nessecary knowledge and tools to jumpstart the development of future charts should you like to create more.

While the worked examples are provided in the repository, it is strongly encouraged that you create your own file and type in the codes yourself to familarize yourself with the development flow.

Note from author

Hi, I'm TK, I had been a Rhythm Recall player since some time around 2022 and was invited into the development team later the same year. Contrary to public belief, this is how I first learnt about C#, I never knew anything about it beforehand aside from GML. For the first few months, I focused on implementing quality of life (QoL) updates to UF-Ex from my experience as a player. While I was just starting off in C#, I noticed that the organization and optimization of UF-Ex was horrible, it didn't even have actual tutorials except for a few .pdf files from Tlott that was of little help, and the structure of UF-Ex was nowhere near the tutorial from MonoGame.

Eventually T-mas, the original creator, abandoned the project and moved over to develop Undyne Fight Role Play Game (UFRPG), and due to the overwhelmingly negative receptions, he open sourced the entire source code of Rhythm Recall without asking any other members. My feelings aside, that provided me motivation to improve UF-Ex as a whole by cutting down reduntant codes and speed up the development cycle using my position in the community.

After two years of hard work on maintaining UF-Ex, I decided that it can be open sourced since the codes are now somewhat presentable, though still hard to pick up due to the lack of documentation. I understand that charting using UF-Ex can be difficult at first, considering I had been there before, so I hope to teach other people how to use UF-Ex to its full extent with this documentation, and that people won't spend far too much time on things that can be easily done.

I hope that from this documentation, you can learn more about UF-Ex and I can learn more about maintaining a project. Happy coding!

  • TK (I am very tired.)
